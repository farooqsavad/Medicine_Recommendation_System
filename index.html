Recommended Precautions

Essential preventive measures for your condition

Maintain Hand Hygiene

Wash hands frequently with soap and water for at least 20 seconds, especially before eating and after using the bathroom.

Wear Protective Masks

Use masks in crowded places to prevent airborne transmission of infectious agents.

Maintain Social Distance

Keep at least 6 feet distance from others, especially those showing symptoms of illness.

Adequate Rest

Ensure 7-8 hours of quality sleep to boost immune function and aid recovery.

Stay Hydrated

Drink at least 8-10 glasses of water daily to maintain proper bodily functions.
